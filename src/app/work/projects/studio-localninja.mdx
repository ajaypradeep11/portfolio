## Overview

[LocalNinja Studio](https://studio.localninja.ca/) is the company website for our Canadian software studio. It presents a broad technical practice - AI chatbots and voice agents, websites, ecommerce, custom software, IT consulting, and maintenance - through one focused story: talk, quote, prototype, build, test, and deliver.

The site needed to feel direct and memorable while making a wide service offering easy to understand. The result uses a crisp neo-brutalist visual system, compact navigation, clear service descriptions, a transparent six-step process, an interactive project catalogue, team profiles, and an uncomplicated contact path.

## Key Features

- **Service-led homepage**: Introduces the studio, its capabilities, and its delivery process without forcing visitors through separate marketing funnels.
- **Expandable service catalogue**: Detailed panels explain AI agents, web and ecommerce development, custom software, consulting, support, automation, cloud, design, and staff augmentation.
- **Transparent delivery process**: Six concrete stages show prospective clients what happens next and what they receive at every milestone.
- **Filterable work gallery**: Live products and interactive demonstrations can be explored by UI/UX, web development, ecommerce, AI, chatbot, and voice-bot capabilities.
- **Studio and team story**: Connects the brand to the people doing the work while keeping the small-team operating model visible.
- **Focused contact experience**: A simple brief form prepares an email so a visitor can start a project without creating an account.
- **Responsive visual system**: Bold typography, high-contrast colour, structured cards, and mobile navigation keep the identity consistent across screen sizes.

## Technologies Used

- **Semantic HTML** for accessible navigation, content hierarchy, service details, project filters, team information, and contact controls.
- **Modern CSS** for the responsive grid, neo-brutalist cards, high-contrast themes, animated marquee, section transitions, and mobile layouts.
- **Modular JavaScript** for service accordions, filtering, navigation state, reveal effects, and lightweight page interactions.
- **Syne and Manrope** for the expressive display typography and readable interface copy.
- **Static asset delivery** for a fast, low-maintenance public site with cacheable JavaScript, CSS, images, and SVG branding.

## What I Built

- Shaped the site architecture around the questions a prospective client asks: what the studio does, how a project runs, what has been built, who is involved, and how to start.
- Developed the LocalNinja visual language with oversized typography, electric blue highlights, heavy outlines, offset shadows, and deliberately simple illustration.
- Built the service and process sections so complex technical work reads as a clear business offering.
- Created the filterable work catalogue that connects the studio story to live products, product screens, and hands-on demonstrations.
- Implemented responsive navigation and layouts across the homepage, work gallery, team section, and contact experience.
- Connected the studio site to LocalNinja's wider product ecosystem, including the ecommerce store and learning platform.

## Challenges and Learnings

The central challenge was breadth. LocalNinja works across AI, product engineering, ecommerce, consulting, and support, but listing every capability with equal weight would make the studio feel unfocused. Organizing the content around outcomes and a repeatable delivery process gives the range a coherent shape.

The second challenge was balancing personality with trust. The sharp blue-and-black system makes the brand recognizable, while the restrained layout, concrete deliverables, live work, named team members, and direct contact route provide the proof a business buyer needs.

## Outcome

LocalNinja Studio is live at [studio.localninja.ca](https://studio.localninja.ca/) as the main presentation layer for the software business. It brings the company's services, operating process, portfolio, team, products, and enquiry flow into one responsive experience and gives each live product a clear connection back to the studio that built it.
